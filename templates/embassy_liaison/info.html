KCK helps Kenyans in Korea access embassy services through guidance, document review, and appointment coordination.

How It Works

A simple 4-step process from request to collection.

1. Submit Your Request

Fill in our online form with your details and upload supporting documents.

2. KCK Review

A KCK officer reviews your request within 1-3 business days.

3. Forward to Embassy

Once verified, we forward your request to the Kenyan Embassy in Seoul.

4. Track & Collect

Track progress in your dashboard and collect your document once ready.

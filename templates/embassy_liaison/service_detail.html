What happens after you submit

  1. Your request enters our queue and is assigned to a KCK officer.
  2. The officer reviews your documents and may contact you if anything is missing.
  3. Once verified, we forward your request to the Embassy of Kenya in Seoul.
  4. You can track status updates in your dashboard anytime.
  5. We will notify you when the document is ready for collection.

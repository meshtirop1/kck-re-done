How it works

Four simple steps from signup to active membership.

1
Sign in & apply

One click to create your application. We generate a unique reference code for you.

2
Transfer the fee

Send the annual fee to the KCK bank account — include your reference code in the transfer description.

3
Declare payment

Come back and click "I've paid". Upload your receipt so the treasurer can match it.

4
Get your member number

The treasurer verifies against the bank statement. You're issued a member number & valid for 12 months.

Anti-fraud note: Benefits activate only after the treasurer confirms the payment against the KCK bank statement. Every action is audit-logged. Membership auto-expires after 12 months — renew to continue.

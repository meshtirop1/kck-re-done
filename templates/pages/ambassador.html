About the Ambassador of Kenya to Korea

The Ambassador is the senior representative of the Republic of Kenya in the Republic of Korea, appointed by the Government of Kenya.

The Ambassador leads the Embassy of Kenya in Seoul, represents Kenya in diplomatic engagements with the Korean government, and oversees official consular services for Kenyan citizens residing in or visiting Korea.

KCK (Kenya Community in Korea) is a community organisation — separate from the embassy — that supports the diaspora community with events, welfare, and embassy-liaison assistance. For official current Ambassador details, please consult the Embassy of the Republic of Kenya in Seoul or the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Kenya.

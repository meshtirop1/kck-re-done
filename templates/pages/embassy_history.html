Kenya & Korea

A journey of diplomacy, partnership, and enduring friendship between Kenya and Korea — and the community that's grown here because of it.

KCK is a community organisation, not the Kenyan Embassy. This page offers general historical context about Kenya–Korea relations and the Kenyan community in Korea. The official Embassy of the Republic of Kenya in Seoul is a separate government institution.

Kenya & Korea — A Long Friendship

Diplomatic relations between the Republic of Kenya and the Republic of Korea were formally established in 1964, just one year after Kenya gained independence. This early recognition marked the beginning of a partnership that has grown steadily over more than six decades.

In those early years, both nations were navigating the challenges of nation-building and economic development. The mutual recognition reflected a shared vision of self-determination, international cooperation, and peaceful coexistence in a rapidly changing world.

Milestones in Bilateral Cooperation

  • 1964 — Kenya and South Korea establish diplomatic relations.
  • 1980s — Expansion of economic cooperation and trade missions between the two countries.
  • 1990s — Launch of educational exchange programs and Korean development assistance initiatives in Kenya.
  • 2000s — Strengthening of high-level political dialogue and bilateral agreements.
  • 2010s — Deepening of investment ties and cultural cooperation, including heritage exhibitions and arts exchanges.
  • Today — A modern partnership focused on technology, sustainable development, and people-to-people connections.

The Kenyan Community in Korea

Alongside the formal diplomatic relationship, a vibrant community of Kenyans has taken root in Korea — students pursuing degrees, professionals working in diverse industries, entrepreneurs building businesses, athletes competing in Korean leagues, and families making Korea home.

From the earliest Kenyan students who arrived in Korea to the thriving diaspora of today, Kenyans in Korea have built networks of support, celebration, and advocacy. Kenya Community in Korea (KCK) grew out of this spirit — a volunteer-run community organisation, by and for Kenyans in Korea.

About KCK

KCK exists to connect and serve Kenyans living in Korea. We organise community events and cultural celebrations, run welfare programmes for members in need, issue community certificates and endorsements, and facilitate liaison with the Kenyan Embassy when members need help with official consular matters.

We are not affiliated with the Kenyan or Korean government, and we do not issue official documents. What we offer is community, support, and a trusted bridge between Kenyans in Korea and the institutions that serve us.

Looking Forward

As the Kenya–Korea partnership continues to evolve — across technology, green energy, education and global cooperation — the Kenyan community in Korea grows alongside it. KCK looks to the future with optimism, building on a strong foundation of friendship and shared goals.

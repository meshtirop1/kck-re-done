Looking for the Kenyan Embassy?

For official government services — passport issuance, visa stamping, authentication, emergency travel documents and similar matters — you need to contact the Embassy of the Republic of Kenya in Seoul.

KCK is not the embassy and cannot issue official documents. However, we can help you prepare your paperwork and coordinate a request via our Embassy Liaison service.

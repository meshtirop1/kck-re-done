Passport Services

Apply for new passports, renewals, and replacements through the embassy

New Passport

First-time applicants for Kenyan passports must provide proof of citizenship, biometric data, and supporting documents.

Required Documents:
  • Completed application form
  • Birth certificate
  • National ID card
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Payment of applicable fees

Passport Renewal

Renew your expiring or expired Kenyan passport while residing abroad. Submit your request in advance to avoid travel disruptions.

Required Documents:
  • Current or most recent passport
  • Completed renewal form
  • Proof of residency in Korea
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Renewal fee payment

How It Works

Submit Application

Fill out the online passport application form with your details.

Upload Documents

Attach scanned copies of supporting documents for verification.

Biometric Capture

Visit the embassy for fingerprints and photograph capture.

Collect Passport

Receive a notification once your passport is ready for pickup.

Common Visa Issues

Troubleshoot common problems and find resolutions

Processing Delays

Applications may take longer than the standard processing time during peak seasons or if additional verification is needed.

Resolution Steps:
  • Check your application status online
  • Ensure all documents were submitted correctly
  • Contact the embassy if processing exceeds stated timeframe
  • Respond promptly to any requests for additional information
Application Rejections

Visas can be rejected due to incomplete documentation, ineligibility, or failure to meet specific requirements.

Resolution Steps:
  • Review the rejection notice for specific reasons
  • Gather missing or corrected documentation
  • Consult with our team before reapplying
  • Submit an appeal if you believe the decision was incorrect
Payment Errors

Payment processing issues can occur due to card declines, incorrect amounts, or technical errors during submission.

Resolution Steps:
  • Verify your payment method has sufficient funds
  • Check transaction records for duplicate charges
  • Retain payment confirmation receipts
  • Contact support with the reference number
Document Verification Issues

Documents may be flagged for illegibility, expiration, or missing authentication stamps.

Resolution Steps:
  • Resubmit clear, high-resolution scans
  • Ensure documents are currently valid
  • Obtain proper notarization where required
  • Translate non-English documents officially
Identity Verification Problems

Mismatched names, dates, or biographic details between your passport and application form can halt processing.

Resolution Steps:
  • Double-check all entries match your passport exactly
  • Submit a name-change affidavit if applicable
  • Provide supporting identity documents
  • Request an amendment through the embassy
Portal Access Issues

Login failures, session timeouts, and form submission errors can disrupt your online application experience.

Resolution Steps:
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies
  • Try a different browser or device
  • Reset your password if login fails
  • Contact technical support for persistent issues
